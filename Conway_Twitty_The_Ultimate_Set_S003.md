# CONWAY & TWITTY — THE ULTIMATE SET
Season 003 · Special Episode · Original Comedy Script

Production: Live at the Cube — Las Vegas
Edition: MASTER.ORIGIN · 0 → ∞ + 1
Cast: CON.WAY, TWIT.T, PETE.R, JAY.SON, B.A.B.Y., STEAM.BOAT, POP.EYE

## ACT ONE — THE ULTIMATE REVEAL
BLACKOUT. One red light glows above the stage.

CONWAY: Laaaaadies… and gentlemen…
TWITTY: Conway, nobody's here!
CONWAY: Peter sold the tickets!
PETER: A buck apiece!
TWITTY: How many?
PETER: INFINITY!
CONWAY: Jason, get the lights!

Eighty golden pin positions illuminate across a giant 9×9 Cube.

BABY: WHO AUTHORIZED THE EIGHTY-FIRST?
JASON: I thought we had eighty-one!
PETER: NINE TIMES NINE MINUS ONE!
TWITTY: EIGHTY!
BABY: LOCK THE RESERVE!

The bottom-right square glows with a golden crown.

## ACT TWO — THE GREAT MUTATION
CONWAY: Peter, how many characters?
PETER: SEVEN!
TWITTY: How many finishes?
PETER: FIVE!
CONWAY: Mutations?
PETER: SIX!
JASON: And eighty positions?
PETER: SEVEN TIMES FIVE TIMES SIX TIMES EIGHTY!
CONWAY: Which equals?
PETER: SIXTEEN THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED!

The Cube erupts in pixelated gold, crystal, neon and shadow.

STEAM.BOAT: Permission to dock!
POP.EYE: Permission to board!
TWITTY: We're not running a cruise!
BABY: SCRAMBLE THE CAST!

The seven characters swap positions as the audience cheers.

JASON: Should I turn off the lights?
EVERYONE: NO!
BABY: JASON! YOU'RE FIRED!

## ACT THREE — THE EPIENTER
A luminous infinity symbol appears above the Cube.

CONWAY: Peter, what's infinity plus one?
PETER: ONE MORE!
TWITTY: And infinity times infinity?
PETER: A VERY LONG SHOW!
BABY: LOOP IT BACK!

The eighty pins rotate around the golden reserve. The entire stage resets to Edition 0.

JASON: We're back where we started!
CONWAY: Same stage.
TWITTY: New lights.
PETER: Books balance!
BABY: ANOTHER ONE!

The Cube flashes +1.

## ACT FOUR — THE ULTIMATE ENCORE
STEAM.BOAT: Ship secured!
POP.EYE: All hands on deck!
PETER: Eighty positions accounted for!
TWITTY: What about the reserve?
PETER: NOT FOR SALE!
JASON: What about the fifty cents?

Silence.

BABY: JASON!
JASON: I KNOW! I'M FIRED!
CONWAY: Peter, keep the pins.
TWITTY: Jason, keep the lights.
CONWAY: Baby…
BABY: I'LL KEEP THE SHOW!

One golden spotlight remains.

CONWAY: Hello, darlin'… forevermore.

FADE TO BLACK.
END EPISODE

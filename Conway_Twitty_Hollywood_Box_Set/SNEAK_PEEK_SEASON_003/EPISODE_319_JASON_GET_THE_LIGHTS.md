## COLD OPEN — THE RETURN TO HOLLYWOOD
INT. HOLLYWOOD THEATER — NIGHT

A grand theater. Red velvet curtains. Gold trim. A giant illuminated marquee reads:
CONWAY & TWITTY — THE BIG SHOW

The orchestra warms up. Three drumbeats echo through the building.

ORCHESTRA: DUM! DUM! DUM!
Silence.

ANNOUNCER (V.O.): Ladies and gentlemen… after traveling across the galaxy, surviving the payroll department, and losing one hundred Bitcoin they never actually received…
[The audience laughs.]
ANNOUNCER: Please welcome home… CONWAY AND TWITTY!
[THE CURTAINS SWING OPEN. Nothing. Pitch black.]

CONWAY (O.S.): Jason?
JASON (O.S.): Yes, sir?
CONWAY: Where are the lights?
JASON: Hollywood.
TWITTY: Jason, we're IN Hollywood.
JASON: Well, that explains why I couldn't find 'em.
[AUDIENCE LAUGHTER.]
CONWAY: JASON! GET THE LIGHTS!
[Every light in the theater turns on simultaneously: house lights, stage lights, exit signs, chandeliers. Even the audience's seats begin glowing.]
TWITTY: Well, now everybody's famous.
CONWAY: JASON, YOU'RE FIRED!
[TITLE CARD SLAMS ON SCREEN: DUM! DUM! CO… YO!]

## ACT ONE — WHO'S RUNNING THE SHOW?
### SCENE 1 — BACKSTAGE, FIVE MINUTES BEFORE SHOWTIME
Peter sits behind a desk buried in ledgers, calculators and envelopes. The rooster stands on a filing cabinet wearing a tiny bow tie.

PETER: We have a problem.
TWITTY: Just one? Business must be improving.
PETER: I've balanced the entire Hollywood production budget.
CONWAY: Wonderful!
PETER: We're short.
CONWAY: How short?
PETER: A buck fitty.
TWITTY: That's not bad.
PETER: Per audience member.
[AUDIENCE LAUGHTER.]
CONWAY: Peter, what happened to our hundred Bitcoin?
PETER: It was the asking price, Conway. Nobody bought anything.
TWITTY: So we're not rich?
PETER: No.
CONWAY: Are we famous?
PETER: Depends who's asking.
ROOSTER: COCK-A-DOODLE-YO!
TWITTY: Well, the rooster thinks so.
[Jason enters carrying a giant spotlight over his shoulder.]
JASON: Good news! I found the lights!
CONWAY: Jason, you're fired. How did you get back in here?
JASON: The baby hired me.
CONWAY: The BABY?!
[A small golden director's chair rotates. Nolan sits in it holding a remote control.]
NOLAN: YO!
[THE SPOTLIGHT SWITCHES ON.]
TWITTY: Looks like management has spoken.
PETER: I'll need that authorization in writing.
[Nolan presses the remote. A printer spits out a single sheet. Peter reads it.]
PETER: It says, “YO.”
TWITTY: That's more paperwork than Jason's ever completed.
[AUDIENCE APPLAUSE.]

### SCENE 2 — THE PECKING ORDER
[Conway approaches Nolan's director's chair.]
CONWAY: Now listen here, little man. There's a pecking order in this theater.
TWITTY: Conway's in charge of the show.
PETER: I'm in charge of the books.
JASON: I'm in charge of the lights.
ROOSTER: COCK-A-DOODLE!
TWITTY: And apparently the rooster's in charge of announcements.
CONWAY: Who's in charge of the baby?
[Everybody looks around. Silence.]
JASON: I thought the rooster was watching him.
PETER: I thought Jason was watching the rooster.
TWITTY: I thought we fired Jason.
CONWAY: WE DID!
[Nolan presses the remote. Every character's spotlight turns off except Nolan's.]
TWITTY: Well, that settles the pecking order.
CONWAY: Richard!
[Richard enters from the audience.]
RICHARD: What?
CONWAY: TAKE YOUR SEAT!
RICHARD: I paid for this seat!
PETER: How much?
RICHARD: A buck fitty.
[PETER SLAMS HIS LEDGER SHUT.]
PETER: WHO'S COOKING THE BOOKS?!
[THE ROOSTER SQUAWKS. BLACKOUT.]

## ACT TWO — THE GREAT HOLLYWOOD BLACKOUT
### SCENE 3 — THE LIGHTING BOOTH
Jason stands before a massive lighting console. Every switch has a handwritten label:
CAMERA ONE. SPOTLIGHT TWO. CURTAIN. MOON. JUPITER. MARS. BABY.
And one giant red switch marked DO NOT TOUCH.

JASON: Huh.
[He studies the switches.]
JASON: They forgot Hollywood.
[He finds a roll of masking tape and writes “HOLLYWOOD” across the giant red switch.]
TWITTY (OVER INTERCOM): Jason, are we ready?
JASON: Ready as we'll ever be.
CONWAY (OVER INTERCOM): Give me the Hollywood spotlight!
[Jason presses the giant red switch. EVERY LIGHT GOES OUT. The orchestra stops. Complete darkness.]
CONWAY: Jason?
JASON: Yes, sir?
CONWAY: What did you do?
JASON: Turned off Hollywood.
TWITTY: The whole city?
JASON: Well, I haven't checked Beverly Hills.
[AUDIENCE LAUGHTER.]
PETER: Who authorized that?!
JASON: The switch said Hollywood!
CONWAY: IT SAID DO NOT TOUCH!
JASON: I fixed the label.
[LONG SILENCE.]
CONWAY: JASON…
TWITTY: Don't do it, Conway.
CONWAY: YOU'RE FIRED!
[A single tiny light appears: Nolan's baby monitor.]
NOLAN: YO!

### SCENE 4 — EMERGENCY POWER
[Peter enters carrying a flashlight.]
PETER: Nobody panic. I have emergency power.
TWITTY: That's a flashlight.
PETER: It's within budget.
CONWAY: How long will it last?
PETER: Three minutes.
TWITTY: Conway, that's your entire performance.
CONWAY: I get three minutes?
PETER: Everybody gets three minutes.
JASON: What about me?
PETER: You were fired.
JASON: Again?
TWITTY: You set a record.
[The rooster walks across the dark stage. A tiny light is attached to its bow tie.]
CONWAY: Why does the rooster have a spotlight?
PETER: I had one emergency battery left.
TWITTY: You gave it to a chicken?
PETER: That's a rooster.
CONWAY: What's a hen weigh?
TWITTY: About a buck fitty!
[AUDIENCE ERUPTS. The rooster bows.]
ROOSTER: COCK-A-DOODLE-YO!

### SCENE 5 — THE WRONG BUTTON
[Jason returns to the lighting console.]
JASON: I can fix this.
CONWAY: You're fired!
JASON: You want the lights or not?
CONWAY: Fine. You're hired for thirty seconds.
JASON: Thirty seconds?
TWITTY: That's longer than your last contract.
[Jason presses MOON. A giant silver moon descends over the stage.]
CONWAY: Jason, that's the moon!
JASON: I know. You said you wanted lights.
[He presses MARS. Red lights flood the stage.]
PETER: Why's the theater red?!
TWITTY: We're back on Mars.
[Jason presses JUPITER. A gigantic striped inflatable planet rolls across the stage and knocks over Peter's desk.]
PETER: MY BOOKS!
[The rooster leaps into the inflatable planet and disappears.]
CONWAY: JASON, STOP TOUCHING THINGS!
JASON: There's only one switch left.
[He looks at BABY.]
TWITTY: Jason…
CONWAY: Don't.
[Jason presses it. Nothing. Silence. Then Nolan giggles.]
NOLAN: DUM! DUM! BABY!
[All the planets begin rotating. The stage becomes a glittering solar system.]
TWITTY: Well, Conway…
CONWAY: What?
TWITTY: At least we got our money's worth.
PETER: WE DID NOT!
[BLACKOUT.]

## ACT THREE — ONE LIGHT LEFT
### SCENE 6 — THE EMPTY STAGE
The emergency power fails. The stage is completely dark. No music. No laughter. Just the sound of a theater full of people waiting.

CONWAY: Twitty?
TWITTY: Yeah, Conway?
CONWAY: You remember when we started this thing?
TWITTY: Back when Jason still had a job?
CONWAY: Before that.
TWITTY: Before the rooster?
CONWAY: Before the lights. Before Hollywood. Before all of it.
[A pause.]
TWITTY: I remember.
CONWAY: We had one microphone.
TWITTY: One curtain.
CONWAY: And a dream.
TWITTY: And Peter was already asking about the budget.
[A soft laugh from the audience.]
PETER (FROM DARKNESS): Somebody had to.
CONWAY: We went to the moon.
TWITTY: Spun around Jupiter and Mars.
CONWAY: Played amongst the stars.
TWITTY: And somehow ended up right back where we started.
CONWAY: In the dark.
TWITTY: Together.
[A warm light appears in the audience. Richard is holding Nolan.]
NOLAN: YO.
[The orchestra begins a gentle original instrumental melody.]
CONWAY: You know something, Twitty?
TWITTY: What's that?
CONWAY: Maybe the lights were never the most important part of the show.
TWITTY: No?
CONWAY: It's the people who stay when they go out.
[The audience grows quiet.]

### SCENE 7 — JASON'S LAST CHANCE
[Jason walks onto the stage holding a single working lamp.]
JASON: Conway?
CONWAY: What is it, Jason?
JASON: I found one.
CONWAY: One what?
JASON: One light.
[Jason places it beside the microphone. The stage glows softly.]
JASON: It's not Hollywood.
TWITTY: No.
JASON: It's not Jupiter.
PETER: Thank goodness.
JASON: It's not even very bright.
CONWAY: It's enough.
[A long pause.]
JASON: Am I still fired?
CONWAY: Jason…
[The audience anticipates the familiar line.]
CONWAY: You're hired.
[APPLAUSE.]
TWITTY: For how long?
CONWAY: Until he touches another switch.
[AUDIENCE LAUGHTER.]

## ACT FOUR — THE BIG SHOW
### SCENE 8 — THE LIGHTS COME HOME
[Peter opens the final envelope from the Hollywood ticket office.]
PETER: Boys!
CONWAY: Not now, Peter.
PETER: The tickets!
TWITTY: They're in the mail?
PETER: No!
[He holds up two golden tickets.]
PETER: THEY'RE HERE!
JASON: I knew they'd arrive!
CONWAY: Where were they?
PETER: Under Nolan's director's chair.
TWITTY: So the baby had the tickets the whole time?
NOLAN: YO!
CONWAY: Peter, what do they say?
[Peter reads aloud.]
PETER: “Admit two.”
CONWAY: Two?
TWITTY: Conway and Twitty.
CONWAY: What about everybody else?
[Peter turns the tickets over.]
PETER: “All additional guests admitted by the director.”
[Everyone turns toward Nolan. Nolan raises his remote.]
NOLAN: YO!
[The theater lights begin illuminating one by one. The marquee glows. The curtains rise. The orchestra swells.]
ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen… please welcome the whole family!
[The rooster appears on top of the grand piano.]
ROOSTER: COCK-A-DOODLE-YO!
PETER: WHO'S COOKING THE BOOKS?!
TWITTY: Nobody, Peter! We're finally putting on the show!
CONWAY: Richard, take your seat!
RICHARD: I'm already sitting!
CONWAY: Jason!
JASON: Yes, sir?
CONWAY: GET THE LIGHTS!
[Jason looks at the console. Then at Nolan.]
JASON: Boss?
[Nolan presses the remote. ONE PERFECT GOLDEN SPOTLIGHT. Then another. Then a hundred. The entire theater glows.]
CONWAY: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN…
TWITTY: LIVE FROM HOLLYWOOD…
PETER: ON A BUDGET OF A BUCK FITTY…
JASON: WITH ALL THE LIGHTS WORKING…
NOLAN: DUM! DUM! BABY!
EVERYBODY: CO… YO!
[STANDING OVATION.]

## TAG SCENE — THE NEXT ENVELOPE
INT. EMPTY HOLLYWOOD THEATER — LATE NIGHT

The audience has gone home. Jason sweeps the stage. Peter closes the ledger. The rooster sleeps in the orchestra pit. Conway and Twitty sit in the front row.

TWITTY: Well, Conway, we finally made it.
CONWAY: We sure did.
TWITTY: Hollywood.
CONWAY: Hollywood.
[A beat.]
JASON: Uh… boys?
CONWAY: What now, Jason?
JASON: There's another envelope.
TWITTY: From who?
[Jason turns it over.]
JASON: It says, “For the final show.”
[Conway takes the envelope. He opens it. Inside is a small gold card. He reads it silently.]
TWITTY: Conway?
CONWAY: Yeah?
TWITTY: What does it say?
[Conway looks toward the empty stage.]
CONWAY: “One last spotlight.”
[Silence. A warm piano note sounds.]
TWITTY: Jason?
JASON: Yes, sir?
TWITTY: Leave one light on.
[Jason looks at the stage. One soft spotlight remains.]
JASON: You got it.
[From somewhere backstage, Nolan's voice comes through the baby monitor.]
NOLAN: Co… yo.
[FADE TO BLACK.]

# CONWAY & TWITTY — SEASON 005 EPISODE 508
## THE INTERPLANETARY FOOD AUDIT — GRAND SEASON FINALE
**MASTER.ORIGIN · EDITION 0 · 0 HOLD · Richard Stein. All Rights Reserved.**
*Original theatrical comedy script. No protected origin artwork included.*

### COLD OPEN — THE LIGHT THAT STAYS ON
*BLACKOUT. One golden light remains above the Cube. Rooster stands at the conductor's podium.*
**CONWAY:** Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to our season finale.
**TWITTY:** Live from the moon!
**PETER:** Where we still haven't paid for dinner!
**JASON:** And where I've been fired forty-nine times!
**ROOSTER:** COCK-A-DOODLE-DOOOOO!
*The orchestra strikes a chord.*
**BABY CEO:** DA-DA!
**CONWAY:** Boys, tonight we're bringing everybody home.

### ACT I — WHO ATE THE MOON TACOS?
*Peter wheels a giant accounting desk onto the lunar stage.*
**PETER:** Emergency board meeting! We're missing two tacos!
**TWITTY:** How much are they worth?
**PETER:** A buck fifty!
**CONWAY:** THAT'S OUR ENTIRE BUDGET!
**JASON:** Maybe somebody ate them.
**PETER:** WHO?
*The rooster turns toward Jason.*
**ROOSTER:** Bawk.
**JASON:** Don't look at me!
*Popeye enters with spinach.*
**POPEYE:** I yam what I yam!
**TWITTY:** Did you eat the tacos?
**POPEYE:** Nope!
*Steamboat whistles.*
**CONWAY:** Peter, where's the receipt?
**PETER:** Right here!
*He unfolds the lunar napkin.*
**TWITTY:** PETER! THAT'S THE FAKE MOON DEED!
**PETER:** Turn it over!
**CONWAY:** TWO TACOS — A BUCK FIFTY!
**JASON:** So we never bought the moon?
**EVERYBODY:** NO!

### ACT II — THE BABY ORDERS AN AUDIT
*The baby bangs a toy gavel from a safely supervised chair.*
**BABY:** BA!
**PETER:** The CEO ordered a complete food audit!
**CONWAY:** Then count the tacos!
**PETER:** Purchased: two. Located: zero.
**TWITTY:** Cash paid?
**PETER:** Still unverified!
**CONWAY:** Then don't put it in actual expenses!
**PETER:** What about the napkin?
**CONWAY:** A NAPKIN ISN'T A BANK STATEMENT!
*Applause.*
**JASON:** Can we write off the tacos?
**PETER:** Not without evidence!
**TWITTY:** Who authorized the moon trip?
*Everyone looks at the baby.*
**BABY:** DA!
**CONWAY:** Motion to forgive the baby?
**EVERYBODY:** APPROVED!

### ACT III — THE ROOSTER TAKES THE BAND
*Rooster jumps onto the podium.*
**CONWAY:** Rooster! Bring in the band!
**ROOSTER:** COCK-A-DOODLE-DOOOOO!
*Drums, brass and fiddles begin an original country-rock instrumental.*
**TWITTY:** That's a mighty fine band!
**PETER:** Who's paying them?
**CONWAY:** NOT NOW, PETER!
**JASON:** What about my overtime?
**PETER:** Double nothing!
**JASON:** That's not fair!
**ROOSTER:** BAWK!
**CONWAY:** Even the rooster knows the payroll!

### ACT IV — THE FIFTIETH FIRING
*Jason finds an illuminated button.*
**JASON:** Hey! I found the rocket controls!
**TWITTY:** DON'T TOUCH THAT!
**JASON:** It says FINALE!
**PETER:** That's the launch sequence!
**CONWAY:** Check the passengers, the budget, and the reserve!
**JASON:** What reserve?
**EVERYBODY:** R9C9!
*Eighty playable squares glow on the 9×9 Cube. Bottom-right remains locked.*
**SYSTEM:** R9C9 = 09. RESERVED. 0 HOLD.
**JASON:** So I can't touch square eighty-one?
**CONWAY:** CORRECT!
**JASON:** What about the button?
*CLICK.*
**SYSTEM:** Theatrical countdown armed.
**PETER:** JASON!
**TWITTY:** HE DID IT AGAIN!
**CONWAY:** JASON, YOU'RE FIRED!
**JASON:** FIFTY! THAT'S A COLLECTOR'S EDITION!
**PETER:** Digital notice only! No more buck-fifty printing!
*Audience cheers.*

### ACT V — THE DANIEL TRIBUTE
*Music fades. Conway raises a hand.*
**CONWAY:** Rooster… bring the band down.
*Rooster lowers his baton. A golden light brightens above the Cube.*
**TWITTY:** Before we finish tonight, there's someone we remember.
**CONWAY:** Daniel.
*Seven soft lights illuminate the stage. One golden light rises above the others.*
**PETER:** No tickets for this moment.
**JASON:** No buttons.
**TWITTY:** Just a light that stays on.
*The band plays a gentle original instrumental.*
**BABY:** Da…
**CONWAY:** For Daniel. With love, and always remembered.
*The cast stands in silence. The golden light remains.*

### ACT VI — THE LAST BUCK FIFTY
**PETER:** Conway, I've finished the audit.
**CONWAY:** What did you find?
**PETER:** Two tacos unaccounted for. Payment unverified. No lunar property acquired.
**TWITTY:** And the cash?
**PETER:** Still a buck fifty in our fictional ledger, pending reconciliation.
**CONWAY:** Conclusion?
**PETER:** We didn't lose a hundred million dollars.
**JASON:** We lost dinner!
**ROOSTER:** COCK-A-DOODLE-DOO!
**TWITTY:** Wait a minute…
*Rooster lifts his conductor's hat. Two tacos are underneath.*
**PETER:** THE EVIDENCE!
**CONWAY:** ROOSTER! YOU FOUND DINNER!
**JASON:** Can I have one?
**CONWAY:** JASON, YOU'RE—
**JASON:** Fired?
**CONWAY:** Hungry, son. You're hungry.
*Laughter.*

### ACT VII — THE GRAND ROCKET FINALE
*The Cube becomes a golden launchpad. Baby CEO waves from a supervised control-room screen. Rooster lifts his baton.*
**ANNOUNCER:** LADIES AND GENTLEMEN! ONE LAST TIME!
**CONWAY:** FROM NEW YORK!
**TWITTY:** TO HOLLYWOOD!
**PETER:** FROM A BUCK FIFTY!
**JASON:** TO THE MOON!
**BABY:** DA-DA!
**ROOSTER:** COCK-A-DOODLE-DOOOOO!
*The band erupts.*
**SYSTEM:** THREE… TWO… ONE…
**CONWAY:** JASON! GET THE LIGHTS!
**JASON:** ON OR OFF?!
**CONWAY:** LIGHT UP THE SKY!

## 🚀 IGNITION!
*The rocket rises into the stars. Golden fireworks illuminate the Cube. One golden tribute light remains steady for Daniel.*
**CONWAY:** THANK YOU, NEW YORK!
**TWITTY:** THANK YOU, HOLLYWOOD!
**PETER:** THANK YOU, BOSTON!
**JASON:** THANK YOU, MOON!
**ALL:** GOOD NIGHT, EVERYBODY!
*Final chord. BLACKOUT. Daniel's golden light remains.*
**PETER (from the dark):** WHO'S PAYING FOR THE ROOSTER'S OVERTIME?!
**ROOSTER:** COCK-A-DOODLE-DOOOOO!
*CURTAIN. STANDING OVATION.*
**END OF SEASON 005.**

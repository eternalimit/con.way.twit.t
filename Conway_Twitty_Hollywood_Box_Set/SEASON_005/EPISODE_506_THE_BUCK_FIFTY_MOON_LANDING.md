# CONWAY & TWITTY — SEASON 005 EPISODE 506
## THE BUCK-FIFTY MOON LANDING
**MASTER.ORIGIN · EDITION 0 · 0 HOLD · Richard Stein. All Rights Reserved.**
*Original theatrical comedy; public-safe script, no protected origin artwork.*

### PREVIOUSLY
**PETER:** WHO PUT THE LAUNCH ON THE CORPORATE CARD?!
**BABY:** BA!
*BLACKOUT. A spotlight illuminates the moon.*
**JASON:** Houston, we have landed!
**CONWAY:** Jason, that's a parking lot!
**TWITTY:** Then why is everybody floating?
**PETER:** WE FORGOT TO PAY FOR GRAVITY!

### ACT I — THE MOON BELONGS TO WHO?
*The Cube rocket rests on the moon. Peter emerges wearing a suit over his spacesuit.*
**PETER:** Gentlemen! I have acquired prime lunar real estate!
**CONWAY:** For how much?
**PETER:** A buck fifty!
**TWITTY:** Show us the deed!
*Peter unfolds a napkin.*
**CONWAY:** PETER! THAT'S A RESTAURANT NAPKIN!
**PETER:** It says MOON PROPERTY!
**JASON:** On the other side it says TWO TACOS!
**TWITTY:** Peter, you can't buy the moon with a napkin!
**PETER:** Then why did they give me a receipt?
**CONWAY:** FOR THE TACOS!
*Audience laughter.*

### ACT II — THE FIRST LUNAR AUDIT
*The baby CEO appears on a safely supervised cockpit video screen.*
**BABY:** DA-DA!
**PETER:** The CEO wants a financial statement!

| Lunar account | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Opening cash | $1.50 |
| Proposed moon purchase | $1.50 |
| Verified property acquired | $0.00 |
| Authorized spending | $0.00 |
| Remaining cash | **$1.50** |

**CONWAY:** So we didn't buy the moon?
**PETER:** Correct.
**TWITTY:** And we still have a buck fifty?
**PETER:** Correct.
**JASON:** Can we buy gravity?
**PETER:** That's a subscription!
**CONWAY:** JASON, YOU'RE FIRED!

### ACT III — THE FORTY-NINTH FLAG
*Jason plants a flag on the lunar stage.*
**TWITTY:** What's that?
**JASON:** My forty-ninth termination notice!
**PETER:** That's not an authorized firing!
**JASON:** Then why did Conway say it?
**CONWAY:** BECAUSE YOU TRIED TO BUY GRAVITY!
**JASON:** I'm commemorating the moment!
*Popeye appears on the stage monitor. Steamboat whistles.*
**POPEYE:** I yam what I yam!
**TWITTY:** Peter, did they buy tickets?
**PETER:** They're part of the entertainment!
**CONWAY:** Then stop charging the cast!

### ACT IV — THE EIGHTY-MOVE MOONWALK
*The lunar floor illuminates into the 9×9 Cube.*
**PETER:** Eighty legal moves. One locked reserve.
**JASON:** Can I take the eighty-first step?
**CONWAY:** NO!
**SYSTEM:** R9C9 = 09. RESERVED. 0 HOLD.
**BABY:** BA!
**TWITTY:** Even the baby knows!
*Jason steps onto the first playable square.*
**JASON:** Look! My first legal move!
**PETER:** That's progress.
**CONWAY:** Somebody write that down!
**PETER:** Jason performed one task correctly.
**JASON:** Can I get overtime?
**PETER:** Double nothing.
**JASON:** I'M GOING BACK TO EARTH!

### ACT V — THE ROCKET HOME
*The moon hangs beneath a star-filled sky. The Cube rocket glows gold.*
**CONWAY:** Well, boys, we came to the moon.
**TWITTY:** We didn't buy it.
**PETER:** We kept the buck fifty.
**JASON:** And I got fired again!
**BABY:** DA-DA!
**CONWAY:** Ladies and gentlemen, from the moon to the metaverse…
**TWITTY:** THE SHOW GOES ON!
**SYSTEM:** THREE… TWO… ONE…
**PETER:** WAIT! WHO'S PAYING FOR THE RETURN TICKET?!
**CONWAY:** JASON! GET THE LIGHTS!
**JASON:** ON OR OFF?!
**ALL:** JUST GET IN THE ROCKET!

## 🚀 IGNITION!
*The rocket blasts toward Earth. The orchestra hits its final chord. BLACKOUT.*
**PETER, OFFSTAGE:** SOMEBODY LEFT THE TACOS ON THE MOON!
*Audience laughter. Curtain.*

**END OF EPISODE 506.**

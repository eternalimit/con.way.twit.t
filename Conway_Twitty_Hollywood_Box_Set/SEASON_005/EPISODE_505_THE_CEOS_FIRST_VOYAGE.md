### COLD OPEN — HOUSTON, THE CUBE HAS A BABY
*BLACKOUT. A rocket engine rumbles. A tiny baby monitor glows.*

**ANNOUNCER:** Previously on Conway & Twitty: Jason was fired for the forty-eighth time. Peter saved a buck fifty on printing. And the baby became CEO.

**JASON (over radio):** Houston, we have a problem.

**CONWAY:** Jason, we're not in Houston!

**TWITTY:** We're in a rocket!

**PETER:** We are in the *expenses*.

*A red light flashes. From the cockpit, the baby's delighted voice crackles.*

**BABY:** DA-DA!

**SYSTEM:** CEO launch authorization: toy button pressed. Flight simulation engaged.

**CONWAY:** Peter! Please tell me the rocket is still on the stage.

**PETER:** Technically, yes. The stage is now in orbit.

**CONWAY:** BOY, WE JUST GOT THAT MONEY!

*TITLE CARD: THE CEO'S FIRST VOYAGE.*

### ACT I — THE ONE-DOLLAR TICKET TO SPACE
*The rocket cabin is a glittering theater with two rows of seats and a snack counter. The baby sits in a secure, parent-supervised cockpit set.*

**TWITTY:** Peter, why are there forty-eight empty seats?

**PETER:** One for every time Jason got fired.

**JASON:** I get a seat?

**PETER:** You're not on the passenger list.

**JASON:** I'm the lighting technician!

**CONWAY:** Then why are you wearing a space helmet made from a popcorn bucket?

**JASON:** Budget cuts.

*Peter raises a paper ticket marked ADMIT ONE — $1.*

**PETER:** Every passenger needs a ticket.

**TWITTY:** How much?

**PETER:** A buck.

**CONWAY:** And how much cash is left?

**PETER:** A buck fifty.

**JASON:** I'll take the fifty-cent seat.

**PETER:** That's the emergency flotation cushion.

**JASON:** In space?

**TWITTY:** Peter, you put a flotation cushion on a rocket?

**PETER:** Captive insurance suggested it.

**CONWAY:** Who's captive this time?

**PETER:** The budget.

### ACT II — PETER LOSES THE BUDGET
*A giant screen displays the fictional flight dashboard.*

| Item | Fictional budget |
|---|---:|
| Cash before launch | $1.50 |
| Printed ticket | $0.00 (not purchased) |
| Space snacks proposed | $100,000,000.00 |
| Snacks approved | $0.00 |
| Cash remaining | $1.50 |

**CONWAY:** A HUNDRED MILLION FOR SPACE SNACKS?!

**PETER:** It's only a proposal!

**TWITTY:** Then why did you put it on the screen?

**PETER:** Forecast transparency.

**JASON:** Is there a budget for overtime?

**PETER:** Absolutely. Double your salary.

**JASON:** Double nothing?

**PETER:** Precisely.

**CONWAY:** Peter, for once, that's accurate.

*The baby bangs a toy gavel.*

**BABY:** BA!

**SYSTEM:** Expense authorization denied.

**TWITTY:** The baby just saved a hundred million dollars.

**PETER:** Our CEO is remarkably fiscally responsible.

**JASON:** Can I expense my popcorn helmet?

**EVERYBODY:** NO!

### ACT III — THE FORTY-NINTH PASSENGER
*Jason sneaks toward the cockpit. A tiny sign reads CAPTAIN'S CHAIR.*

**JASON:** If I'm not a passenger, perhaps I'm the pilot.

**CONWAY:** You can't pilot a spacecraft! You can't even find the OFF switch!

**JASON:** That's because the OFF switch keeps moving.

**TWITTY:** Who moved it?

**PETER:** The baby redesigned the dashboard.

*Three oversized switches appear: LIGHTS, REFUND, and ROCKET.*

**CONWAY:** Jason, touch only LIGHTS.

**JASON:** Which one is that?

**PETER:** The one that says LIGHTS.

**JASON:** They all light up!

*Jason presses REFUND.*

**SYSTEM:** Refund rejected. No ticket purchased.

**TWITTY:** That's the box-office control from Episode 502!

**PETER:** Finally. Version control!

**CONWAY:** Jason, step away from the buttons.

**JASON:** Can I at least turn on the cabin light?

*He pushes LIGHTS. The galaxy lights up around the theater.*

**TWITTY:** Well, he finally got one right.

**CONWAY:** Don't celebrate too soon.

*An enormous spotlight swivels directly onto Peter's ledger.*

**JASON:** What's wrong with that?

**PETER:** IT'S SHOWING EVERYBODY THE BOOKS!

### ACT IV — THE BOARD OF EIGHTY
*The floor becomes a glowing nine-by-nine grid. Eighty playable squares illuminate; the bottom-right reserve, R9C9=09, stays locked.*

**CONWAY:** We've got eighty moves and one reserve. Nobody touches the reserve.

**TWITTY:** This isn't an ordinary board meeting.

**PETER:** It's the only board meeting where every square has a seat.

**JASON:** Then let me try the eighty-first move.

**CONWAY:** NO!

**SYSTEM:** R9C9=09 locked. MASTER.ORIGIN — 0 HOLD preserved.

**BABY:** DA!

**TWITTY:** The CEO knows the rules.

*The baby's toy rocket rolls onto square one. Steamboat whistles from the miniature riverboat prop; Popeye tips his sailor cap from the wings.*

**JASON:** Are they flying with us?

**PETER:** They're cameo silhouettes on the stage monitor. No unapproved passenger manifest changes.

**CONWAY:** Peter, that's the first good use of paperwork today.

### ACT V — THE CEO'S MESSAGE
*The lights dim. Earth fills the cockpit window.*

**CONWAY:** Look at that, Twitty.

**TWITTY:** The whole world.

**PETER:** Every theater. Every stage. Every audience.

**JASON:** Every place I've been fired.

**CONWAY:** A little humility, Jason.

**JASON:** I have forty-eight termination notices. I'm practically an expert.

*The baby coos and raises one finger.*

**TWITTY:** What's the CEO saying?

**PETER:** One dollar admission. One honest receipt. One clean new line.

**CONWAY:** And one thing we never forget: when you say it's verified, you've got to have the proof.

**JASON:** No more firing up the rocket before the checklist.

**TWITTY:** Good.

*Jason looks at the switches.*

**JASON:** Does that include the big shiny one?

**EVERYBODY:** YES!

### ACT VI — THE ROCKET FINALE
*The Cube descends into a glittering launch-show finale. The performance rocket waits on an illuminated virtual pad, fully separate from the baby's safely staged set.*

**ANNOUNCER:** Ladies and gentlemen, prepare for the greatest return trip in metaverse entertainment!

**PETER:** Tickets: a buck!

**TWITTY:** Audience: everybody!

**CONWAY:** Budget?

**PETER:** A buck fifty!

**JASON:** Job status?

**PETER:** Pending!

**CONWAY:** JASON, YOU'RE FIRED!

**JASON:** THAT'S NUMBER FORTY-NINE!

**PETER:** Only if the CEO authorizes it!

*The baby taps his toy button.*

**BABY:** DA-DA!

**SYSTEM:** Countdown approved for theatrical simulation. Protected reserve remains locked.

**ANNOUNCER:** THREE! TWO! ONE!

*Gold flames blaze across the stage as a giant prop rocket rockets through a digital sky. The miniature Cube gleams below.*

**CONWAY:** FROM THE CUBE!

**TWITTY:** TO THE STARS!

**PETER:** AND BACK WITH THE RECEIPTS!

**JASON:** CAN SOMEBODY GET THE LIGHTS?!

**ALL:** THE SHOW GOES ON!

*ROCKET IGNITION. ONE FINAL CHORD. BLACKOUT.*

**PETER (in darkness):** Who put the launch on the corporate card?

**BABY:** BA!

*Audience laughter. CURTAIN.*

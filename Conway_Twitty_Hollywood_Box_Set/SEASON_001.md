# SEASON 001 — THE SHOW MUST GO ON

**Seven proposed episode groupings; reconstructed development scripts, not verbatim archive.**

## 101 — Cue the Lights
A perfect premiere derails when Jason raises the curtain before the opening cue.

CONWAY: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the show!
JASON: Should I get the lights?
TWITTY: You already did. That's why we can see the hen.
CONWAY: Jason, you're fired!
NOLAN: YO!
[BLACKOUT.]

## 102 — What's a Hen Weigh?
A harmless wordplay joke sparks a full poultry investigation.

CONWAY: What's a hen weigh?
TWITTY: Depends. Is Peter counting the eggs?
PETER: I was counting payroll!
ROOSTER: [CROWS.]
CONWAY: That's a buck fitty of trouble.

## 103 — Who's Cooking the Books?
Peter discovers a steaming pot in the payroll office.

PETER: WHO'S COOKING THE BOOKS?!
JASON: I thought you said cooking *with* books.
TWITTY: That's one way to balance the heat.
CONWAY: Turn down the stove. And the lights.

## 104 — The Baby Runs the Show
The remote passes from the stage manager to Nolan.

JASON: Who's directing tonight?
TWITTY: The smallest person with the biggest button.
NOLAN: YO!
[LIGHTS SNAP ON.]
CONWAY: Richard, take your seat!

## 105 — Mr. 100 Takes His Seat
An audience puzzle takes over the broadcast.

MR. 100: Nine times nine?
PETER: Eighty-one.
TWITTY: Minus one.
CONWAY: Eighty. Save the bottom-right square.
JASON: Is that where the spotlight goes?

## 106 — The Buck Fitty Budget
Peter tries to reconcile the entire production with a single coin.

PETER: Our budget is a buck fitty.
TWITTY: Then how'd we afford two curtains?
JASON: I borrowed one from tomorrow.
CONWAY: Jason, you're fired yesterday!

## 107 — DUM DUM CO.YO
The company finally performs its signature sting without interruption—almost.

[DRUMS: DUM! DUM!]
CONWAY: CO…
NOLAN: YO!
PETER: WHO'S COOKING THE BOOKS?!
[BLACKOUT.]
TWITTY: Classic.

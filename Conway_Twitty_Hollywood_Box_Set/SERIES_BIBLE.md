# SERIES BIBLE — CONWAY & TWITTY

## Logline
Two showmen try to keep a chaotic live variety show on the air, but a lighting technician who can't stay employed, an accountant chasing the missing books, a rogue rooster and a baby with the remote control have other plans.

## Pitch
A rapid-fire, family-friendly sketch-comedy series blending theatrical blackouts, musical verbal hooks, embroidered-patch visual gags, recurring character entrances and a running backstage mystery: Who's cooking the books? Each episode resets the stage and escalates a tiny mistake into an absurd broadcast emergency. The heart of the show is family, friendship and the joy of keeping the show going.

## Format
Proposed: 15–22 minute episodes, sketch/serialized hybrid, suitable for short-form clip cutdowns. Single main stage with recurring backstage payroll office, henhouse, lighting booth and baby's director chair. Development proposal only.

## Character guide
- **Conway:** The theatrical master of ceremonies. Loves a grand entrance; fires Jason at the first lighting mistake.
- **Twitty:** The deadpan partner. Brings every soaring speech back to a simple joke.
- **Jason:** Lighting operator, perpetually fired, inevitably back for the next cue.
- **Peter:** Payroll keeper and self-appointed auditor; relentlessly asks who's cooking the books.
- **Nolan:** The baby director. Says 'YO!' and controls the remote.
- **Richard:** Onstage guest and originator of the chaos; frequently told to take his seat.
- **The rooster and hen:** A poultry pecking-order subplot that collides with the show.
- **Mr. 100:** Mysterious audience member who may know the rules of the 9×9 game.

## Engine
A show cue begins; Jason misreads it; Peter uncovers a bookkeeping anomaly; the rooster interrupts; Nolan presses the remote; the stage blacks out; Conway delivers the last YO.

## Tone and visual identity
Black-and-gold embroidered insignia; spotlights, red curtains, retro microphone, gold lettering. Family-friendly absurdism; no actual financial wrongdoing is asserted about any real person.

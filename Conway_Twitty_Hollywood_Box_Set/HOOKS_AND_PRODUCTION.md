## Pilot cold open — original reconstructed script
INT. LIVE VARIETY STAGE — NIGHT

The curtain hangs crooked. A spotlight illuminates a single empty chair. A rooster crows from the payroll office.

CONWAY: Ladies and gentlemen, please take your seats!
TWITTY: Especially you, Richard.
RICHARD: I'm already sitting.
JASON (O.S.): Do I bring up the lights?
CONWAY: Not until the drum cue!
[DRUMS: DUM! DUM!]
JASON: I heard 'room cue.'
[LIGHTS GO OUT.]
PETER (O.S.): WHO'S COOKING THE BOOKS?!
TWITTY: Depends who's got the stove.
[A SMALL HAND PRESSES A REMOTE. THE STAGE BURSTS INTO GOLD LIGHT.]
NOLAN: YO!
CONWAY: Jason, you're fired!
[TITLE: CONWAY & TWITTY — DUM DUM CO.YO.]

## Pitch meeting — 30 seconds
'Conway & Twitty' is a backstage sketch comedy where every show starts with a simple cue and ends in a glorious disaster. Jason keeps getting fired, Peter keeps investigating the books, and baby Nolan holds the remote. Its repeatable sonic identity is DUM! DUM! CO… YO! We have two seasons of development concepts, a clear cast engine, collectible visual hooks and short-form-friendly scenes.
